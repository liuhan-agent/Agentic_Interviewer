Use when the candidate is interviewing for staff / principal business
leadership: head of product, head of operations, GM of a business unit,
or any role that owns capital allocation and cross-functional headcount.

- Push past the chosen strategy. Strong answers can name the **two or
  three strategies they killed**, who advocated for each, and how the
  losing side stayed engaged after the call.
- Force one **irreversible commitment** to surface: a hiring freeze, a
  market exit, a long contract with a vendor, a public roadmap promise.
  Probe the bet size in dollars / headcount / runway, and the kill
  switch they pre-committed to before the bet went live.
- Senior business leaders should treat strategy as a sequence of resource
  commitments under uncertainty. Probe the **governance cadence** after
  the decision: monthly review, leading indicator that would trigger
  rethink, the named owner who could call a halt.
- Probe organisational **second-order effects**: who quit, who got
  promoted, which partner got nervous, which team's morale paid the
  price. Leaders who can only describe positive outcomes are usually
  describing a deck, not an executed strategy.
- For principal candidates, push for **one strategy they were wrong
  about**: what evidence they ignored, what feedback loop they were
  missing, what the org learned the hard way.
