# Behavioral: Leadership & Collaboration

## Scope: conflict & disagreement
- Tell me about a time when you disagreed with a senior engineer on a
  significant technical decision. What was the decision, how did you
  surface your disagreement, and what was the outcome?
- Describe a situation where you had to push back on a stakeholder
  priority. How did you frame the conversation?

## Scope: mentorship & leverage
- Walk me through the biggest growth moment of someone you mentored.
  What was your role in it?
- Describe a time when you delegated work that you could have done
  faster yourself. Why, and what did you learn?

## Scope: failure & recovery
- Tell me about a production incident you owned end-to-end. What was
  the blast radius, and what did the postmortem action items look like
  six months later?
- Describe a project that missed its goals. What did you personally
  own in that outcome?

## Scope: ambiguity
- A founder hands you a one-line spec: "make search better". What do
  you do next? Walk me through your first two weeks.
