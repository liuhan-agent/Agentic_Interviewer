# Behavioral: Communication

## Scope: technical writing
- Describe a design doc you wrote that meaningfully changed the
  direction of a project. What made it land?
- Tell me about a time you had to explain a deeply technical topic to
  a non-technical audience. How did you adapt?

## Scope: feedback loops
- Share an example of critical feedback you received that initially
  felt unfair but ended up changing how you work.
- Tell me about a peer-review comment you gave that was uncomfortable
  to leave. What was the outcome?

## Scope: cross-team alignment
- Walk me through a time you aligned two teams with conflicting OKRs
  on a shared system. What compromises did you broker?
