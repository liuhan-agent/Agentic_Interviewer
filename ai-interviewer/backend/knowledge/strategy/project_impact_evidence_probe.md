Project experience should be grounded in impact, scope, and the candidate's
specific contribution.

How to apply:
- Ask for the baseline, the change, and the measured or observed result.
- Separate "team delivered" from "I personally owned".
- Ask who used the result and what changed for them.
- If metrics are unavailable, ask for proxy evidence and why that proxy is credible.

Pitfalls:
- Do not accept responsibility lists as impact evidence.
- Avoid over-penalizing early-career candidates for lacking large-scope ownership.
