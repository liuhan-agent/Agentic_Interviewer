Good product answers show how the candidate discovered uncertainty, not only how
they executed a decision.

How to apply:
- Ask what user or business uncertainty existed before the decision.
- Require the evidence used: data, interviews, support tickets, experiments, or stakeholder constraints.
- Ask what option they rejected and why.
- For senior candidates, ask how they aligned stakeholders around the decision.

Pitfalls:
- Do not let the answer become a launch summary without the decision path.
- Avoid judging only by outcome; inspect the quality of evidence and iteration.
