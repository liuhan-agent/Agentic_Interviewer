Product thinking is not just choosing a metric; it is knowing when a metric can
mislead the team.

How to apply:
- Ask which metric the team optimized and what bad behavior it could create.
- Require one guardrail metric or qualitative signal.
- Ask how they would explain the trade-off to a non-technical stakeholder.
- If the candidate is technical, connect the metric to one product or system design decision.

Pitfalls:
- Do not accept generic "conversion rate" answers without a user segment or behavior.
- Avoid product theater; require an action the candidate actually influenced.
