Interview turn budget is limited. When a dimension already has enough evidence,
coverage can be more valuable than another deep follow-up.

How to apply:
- If the previous answer is strong and remaining turns are limited, switch to an uncovered dimension.
- If the current dimension has conflicting evidence, allow one clarification before switching.
- Prefer switching when the next dimension has a resume or question-bank anchor ready.
- Explain the next question through the new dimension, not as a random topic jump.

Pitfalls:
- Do not switch away from a failed answer if the evaluator explicitly requested refinement.
- Do not treat a single fluent answer as complete coverage for a complex dimension.
