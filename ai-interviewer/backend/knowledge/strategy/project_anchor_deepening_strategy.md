When resume anchors are available, use the selected project as the spine of the
question instead of asking abstract knowledge checks.

How to apply:
- Tie the follow-up to the current project anchor and one concrete responsibility.
- Ask for the most important technical decision inside that project.
- Require a before/after or failure/recovery detail.
- If the candidate drifts away from the project, bring them back to the selected anchor.

Pitfalls:
- Do not mention every resume keyword; use one anchor per question.
- Avoid making assumptions about ownership that the candidate has not claimed.
